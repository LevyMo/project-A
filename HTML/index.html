About us

A family wanting to bring community to our city. In something we love. Video games and food. we want to create a place where you can gather freinds and family. To enjoy casual games and competitve games. We especially want to help cultivate the competitve side of gaming as there aren't many ways to get the LAN experience to operate at a PRO level in gaming. So we want to provide that locally, and at an affordable price.

Currently we have no storefront, so we opted to begin selling Puerto Rican pastries to eventually be able to open a storefront. Thats only part 1 of our establishment, as we intend to stream to also help bring in revenue to open the storefront. An important part of the puzzle as we intend to implement streaming into the business to show off our local talent, in hopes of their talents being acknowledged by the world.
