Battle Ready Cafe

About Us

A family wanting to bring community to our city through something we love: video games and food. We want to create a place where friends and family can gather to enjoy both casual and competitive gaming.

We especially want to help cultivate the competitive side of gaming, since there are not many local ways to experience high-level LAN events at a professional level. Our goal is to provide that experience locally and at an affordable price.

Currently we have no storefront, so we began by selling Puerto Rican pastries to help fund our future location. We also plan to stream content to help generate revenue and showcase local gaming talent to the world.
